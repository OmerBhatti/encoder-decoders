Encoder-Decoders

Choose a tool to encode, decode, or hide text in images and audio.

  • Text ↔ RGB Pixel Encoder

    Encode text into PNG pixel data and decode it back to text.

  • Text ↔ WAV Audio Encoder

    Convert text to audio tones and decode tones back to text.

  • Steganography (Text in Image)

    Hide a secret message inside an image and decode it back from pixel bytes.
